About Evidence Action

Evidence Action scales evidence-based and cost-effective programmes to improve lives. The organisation operates at scale and rigorously monitors impact to ensure interventions deliver measurable outcomes for the communities they serve.

In Andhra Pradesh, Evidence Action (EvAc) partners with the state government under the Jal Jeevan Mission to deploy and monitor Inline Chlorination (ILC) devices across rural water supply schemes.

About the ISW Programme

The India Safe Water (ISW) programme ensures continuous, automated chlorination of piped water at the point of distribution — eliminating manual dosing variability and reducing water-borne disease burden.

  • Automated inline chlorination at scheme level
  • Tablet-based calcium hypochlorite dispensing (ILC buckets)
  • Monitoring via TA and LMT field visits
  • Free Residual Chlorine (FRC) & Total Residual Chlorine (TRC) tracked

Our Model

The ILC system operates through a bucket-based dispenser installed at the pump head. Chlorine tablets dissolve as water flows, maintaining safe residual chlorine levels at both first-household (FHH) and last-household (LHH) taps.

Field teams conduct Technical Assessment (TA) and Last-Mile Testing (LMT) visits to verify device performance and water quality compliance.
