Terminology

TermFull Form / Definition
ILCInline Chlorination — automated chlorine dosing at the water source

TRCTotal Residual Chlorine — total of free and combined chlorine in water
FHHFirst Household — tap closest to the water source
LHHLast Household — tap furthest from the water source
TATechnical Assessment — structured field visit by EvAc staff
LMTLast Mile Testing — verification of water quality at end-user taps
GPGram Panchayat — village-level local government body
WSSWater Supply Scheme — the piped water supply infrastructure
ISWIntegrated Safe Water — Evidence Action's water safety programme
JJMJal Jeevan Mission — GoI programme for piped water to every household
EvAcEvidence Action — implementing organisation
